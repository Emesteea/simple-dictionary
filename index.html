A

  • Audacious: Showing a willingness to take surprisingly bold risks, or showing a lack of respect.
    Example: The team made an audacious attempt to solve the complex problem in just one afternoon.
  • Ameliorate: To make something bad or unsatisfactory better.
    Example: The city planners added new parks to help ameliorate the effects of urban pollution.
  • Astute: Having or showing an ability to accurately assess situations or people and turn this to one's advantage.
    Example: Her astute observations during the meeting helped the business avoid a major financial mistake.
  • Alleviate: To make suffering, deficiency, or a problem less severe.
    Example: Taking a warm bath can quickly alleviate muscle soreness after a heavy workout.
  • Anomaly: Something that deviates from what is standard, normal, or expected.
    Example: The sudden drop in temperature during July was a complete weather anomaly.
